Our 2027 Speaker

Tama Kieves

"Follow your rebel brilliance."

Tama Kieves is an honors graduate of Harvard Law School who left a prestigious law career to follow her calling as a writer, speaker, and teacher. She is the bestselling author of six books, including A Course in Miracles for Life Ninjas and her most recent, Learning to Trust Yourself (St. Martin's Press, 2025).

Featured in USA Today, the Huffington Post, Forbes, and on ABC News and Oprah Media, Tama has helped thousands worldwide discover, launch, and thrive in the life and work of their dreams. She has taught A Course in Miracles in an interactive, deeply experiential way for over 30 years.

Tama presents at the Omega Institute, the Kripalu Center for Yoga and Health, the Esalen Institute, 1440 Multiversity, and TEDx, and is known for her electrifying presence, grounded compassion, and the sweeping sense of possibility she ignites in those she teaches.

Retreat Details

When

May 21–23, 2027
Friday evening through Sunday afternoon

Where

Mercy Conference and Retreat Center
St. Louis, Missouri

The Council has held its annual retreat at Mercy Center for many years. It is a beautiful and peaceful setting for spiritual deepening.

Who Attends

ACIM students from St. Louis and beyond. Past retreats have welcomed 50–85 participants. Both in-person and virtual options have been available in recent years.

Refund Policy

We understand that plans change. Our refund policy is designed to be fair and clear for all participants.

Refund window

Refund requests must be submitted at least 3 weeks before the event. No refunds after that date.

Credit card payments

Refunded at the original transaction amount less a 10% processing fee.

Cash or check payments

Refunded in full, provided the request is within the refund window.

Registration transfers

You may transfer your registration to another person up to 1 week before the event. Requests must be made in writing with the new attendee's full name and contact information, and are subject to approval.

No-shows

Participants who register but do not attend are not eligible for a refund.

Partial attendance

No refunds are provided for partial attendance of multi-day or multi-session events.

Multiple attendee registrations

If a registration includes multiple attendees, partial refunds may be requested for individual attendees, provided the request meets all other conditions of this policy.

Event cancellation by ACIM St. Louis

In the rare event the Council must cancel or reschedule, all participants receive a full refund.

Processing time

Refunds are processed within 30 days. Refunds are issued first to the original credit card, then via PayPal, then by check to the address on file.

All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted in writing (email is acceptable) with the registrant's name, event details, and payment method. Questions about registration or refunds? Contact us at acimstlouiscouncil@gmail.com.
